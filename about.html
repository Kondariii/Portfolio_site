About

Jakub Krzemiński

Photographer & Filmmaker based in Łódź, Poland

I'm Jakub Krzemiński, a photographer based in Łódź, Poland. I'm passionate about capturing real moments and telling stories through images. Photography allows me to freeze emotions, atmosphere, and small details that often go unnoticed.

I enjoy working with both photos and video, always looking for creative ways to improve my work and develop my style. Whether it's events, portraits, or everyday scenes, my goal is to create images that feel natural and authentic.
